Mahmoud is a creative, detail-oriented graphic designer with extensive experience across branding, social media design, advertising, and motion graphics. He's spent the past nine years delivering visually compelling content inside fast-paced, deadline-driven environments — most recently with a focus on Gulf-market branding and current design trends.

Day to day, that means leading a team of designers and video editors — and mentoring its juniors — through concept, art direction, and final delivery: building out a brand's visual world, keeping a campaign consistent across formats, and making sure motion and static work speak the same language.

Mahmoud is fully capable of leading a creative team and has held that position before. He managed a team of seven designers and video editors, oversaw roughly 40 clients whose work the team delivered, and was responsible for the quality and consistency of the output. The team included junior members, whom he taught and developed hands-on, and he delivered strong results in the role.
